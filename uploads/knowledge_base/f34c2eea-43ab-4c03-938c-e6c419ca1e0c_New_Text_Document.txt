5. Provisioning SOPs
5.1 FTTH Provisioning

Verify address in Fiber Map System.

Patch port on OLT → service splitter.

Assign ONT to VLAN profile:

VLAN 110 – Internet

VLAN 120 – IPTV

VLAN 130 – VoIP

Activate ONT via OLT CLI:

ont add 1 0 sn-auth XXXX type HG8245H
ont port native-vlan 1 110


Create PPPoE user in RADIUS.

Confirm LOS light and optical power (-24 to -19 dBm).

5.2 Wireless Provisioning

Identify nearest tower.

Align CPE (RSSI target: -55 to -65 dBm).

Configure with:

VLAN 110 Internet

Management VLAN 10

Bind to PPPoE profile.

6. Troubleshooting Guides
6.1 Internet Down (General)

Ping ONT or CPE (10.x.x.x management).

Check LOS on fiber or RSSI on wireless.

Verify PPPoE session on BRAS:

ppp active print | find <username>


Check VLAN tagging on switches.

AT&T drop test: power cycle ONT 30 sec.

6.2 Slow Speeds

Verify router is Gigabit-capable.

Check WiFi interference (channel scan).

Confirm customer not using VPN.

Confirm no router double-NAT.

6.3 IPTV Issues

Packet loss above 2% on VLAN 120.

STB multicast groups not joined.

Restart IGMP proxy.
