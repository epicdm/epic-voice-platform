**Current Model**: Selling to small businesses at $200-$2,500/month
- Need 400 customers to reach $1M ARR
- High support burden (end users)
- Direct competition with ElevenLabs/Vapi

**White-Label Model**: Sell your LiveKit + Magnus + AI stack to:
- **Marketing agencies** ($5K-$15K MRR each)
- **CRM platform partners** ($10K-$50K MRR each)
- **Regional telecom providers** ($20K-$100K MRR each)
- **Vertical SaaS platforms** ($5K-$30K MRR each)
